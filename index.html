THE FRACTURE

The Core Thesis

“We are living through a historic tearing of the American social fabric—not merely political, but generational, economic, geographical, and psychological. The Fracture is an intentional two-volume journey: an unflinching empirical diagnosis of our deepest fault lines (Tracks 1–8), followed by a compassionate, evidence-based roadmap toward vulnerability, community stewardship, and human reconnection (Tracks 9–14).”

Between the echo chamber and the corporate spreadsheet, between the young man stranded in an empty room and the hollowed-out rural hospital, the divide feels insurmountable. Yet the ground we stand upon is identical. The cure begins when we put down the megaphone, acknowledge our grief, and reach across the river.
